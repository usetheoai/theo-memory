### Distance vs similarity convention

LangChain `distance_threshold=0.2` (cosine distance, lower = closer) ≈ embedchain `similarity_threshold=0.8` (cosine similarity, higher = closer), since `cosine_similarity = 1 - cosine_distance`. **STRONG CONVERGENCE**: two canonical libs independently calibrate to the same cosine threshold (0.8 similarity / 0.2 distance) for LLM-recall semantic cache hit.

### Known calibration issue (verbatim from WebSearch)

> "Users configuring `RedisSemanticCache` with `distance_threshold=0.3` and OpenAI embeddings found that semantically similar questions like 'What is the capital of France?' and 'Can you tell me the capital city of France?' were being treated as different cache entries because they generate different embeddings."

**Interpretation**: at `distance_threshold=0.3` (= similarity 0.7), the cache becomes too strict on OpenAI embedding similarities. This is empirical pressure to RELAX the threshold (use 0.2 distance / 0.8 similarity).
