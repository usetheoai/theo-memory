## What shipped

The third signal of the hybrid recall stopped being a constant zero. From v0.1 until this
release, `combineScores` summed an `entity` component that was always `0` — what the docs
called "semantic + BM25 + entity boost" was, in practice, "semantic + BM25".

## Honest notes

The `/review` found more about the quality of my own evidence than about the feature. Two
instruments I built could not detect what they claimed to measure: `boostedCandidateCount`
compared ranks across runs over a baseline I had myself documented as unstable, so a dead boost
could have passed it; and the churn measurement ran at a threshold where renormalization was
arithmetically incapable of biting, even though plan ADR D1 accepted that risk **on the
condition** that D2 would measure it. Both were rebuilt. The corrected churn instrument then
found the effect — 75 evictions at threshold 0.8 — which is documented in the CHANGELOG and
tracked in #127.

A BLOCKER also surfaced that had passed through a full `/review` in M14 unnoticed: two live
`theo_live_*` keys tracked in git, one release cut away from entering `main`'s history. Removed
and gitignored; rotation is tracked in #126, because the keys remain in `develop`'s history and
`git-safety.md` § 2 rules out rewriting it on a shared branch.
